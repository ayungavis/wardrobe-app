— a SegFormer-B4 fine-tuned for human parsing.

- Model: <https://github.com/fashn-AI/fashn-human-parser>
- Weights: <https://huggingface.co/fashn-ai/fashn-human-parser>
- Upstream architecture: NVlabs/SegFormer <https://github.com/NVlabs/SegFormer>
